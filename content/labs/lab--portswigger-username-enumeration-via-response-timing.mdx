## Why This Works (The Principle)
- **The vulnerability:** The authentication mechanism has a logic flaw that allows bypass or credential discovery.
- **Root cause:** Weak brute-force protections, predictable tokens, flawed multi-step logic, or race conditions.
- **Exploitation:** Exploit the specific weakness — enumerate users via timing/lockout, bypass 2FA by skipping steps, crack predictable cookies.
- **Key insight:** Authentication flaws often live in the *gaps* between checks — timing windows, skipped verification steps, or unprotected endpoints.
